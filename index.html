Europe chose CLAM to renew its fishing fleet

CLAM is a referenced industrial partner of the European programme for the renewal and modernisation of the fishing fleet, 2028–2034.

After almost twenty-five years without support, the European Union will again help fishermen replace their old boats. The goals are clear: cleaner and more fuel-efficient boats, safer work on board, and no extra pressure on fish stocks. CLAM was selected for its robust and affordable hulls.

  • Energy efficiencyLighter hulls that need less fuel and are ready for low-carbon engines.
  • Safety on boardReinforced structure, unsinkable foam and non-slip decks.
  • CapacityProduction sized to deliver every order from programme beneficiaries.
  • TransparencyA yearly delivery report sent to the programme, with a mid-term review in 2031.

Partnership ref. PRF-2028-34/FR/0117 · signed in Brussels, 20 November 2026

Why should a good fishing boat be expensive?

Our slogan

Keep CLAM.

In French: « Avec CLAM, on reste calmes ». Our motto: Built for the water. Made for you.

CLAM is a French boat builder based in Toulon. Our moulding and finishing workshops build simple, strong boats that are sold through professional dealers in France and across Europe.

We started with one question and never let it go. Fishermen need a boat they can trust every day, not luxury they will never use. So we focus on what matters: strength, safety and a fair price.

Our philosophy is robust hulls at low prices. Like the great carmakers who made reliable cars affordable for everyone, we apply lean production to boatbuilding: standard parts, simple designs, no waste and constant improvement. The savings go into the price, never out of the quality.

CLAM Telline

Our simplest boat, and proud of it. The Telline is a stripped-back 4-metre hull with three wooden benches and nothing you do not need. Row it, scull it or fit a small outboard: it goes wherever you want to go.

It is built for the keen amateur fisherman, but owners use it for much more: a quiet trip on the lake, a family outing, or getting around marshes and waterways, thanks to its very shallow draft.

Eligible for the European fleet renewal programme.

News from the yard

20.112026 · Brussels

CLAM selected as industrial partner of the European fishing fleet renewal programme

The European programme for the renewal and modernisation of the fishing fleet (2028–2034) has chosen CLAM, the boat maker from Toulon, as one of its referenced industrial partners.

Read more

For the first time in almost twenty-five years, European funds will help fishermen replace their old boats. The aim is to cut fuel use, improve safety on board and keep fishing capacity stable. CLAM and the programme share a common target: at least 25% of the renewed fleet built on CLAM hulls by the end of 2034.

“This partnership proves that a robust boat does not have to be expensive,” said the CLAM management team. “We are proud that Europe trusts a small yard from Toulon.”

CLAM opens its order book for the 2027 season

Dealers can now order the CLAM Telline for the 2027 season. Orders placed before the end of March will be delivered in time for spring.

05.112026 · Toulon

Lean production: how CLAM keeps its prices low

CLAM has reorganised its moulding and finishing workshops around lean production methods. Fewer parts, less waste and shorter build times.

Read more

Inspired by the car industry, CLAM now uses standard components across its hulls and checks every unit at each step instead of at the end of the line. The result: a lower production cost, fewer defects and a price that remains one of the most competitive on the market.
